# 1. Research project - IMT Mines Alès

## 1.1 Theme

How is the popularity of romantic piano pieces influenced by the harmonic musical structures that compose these works ?

The intersection of musicology, data science, and the music industry has given rise to a field of research known as Music Information Retrieval (MIR). The subject presented here is rooted precisely within the sub-discipline called Hit Song Prediction (HSP). The objective is to identify an optimal musical architecture in order to better understand the technical—and more specifically, harmonic—determinants of a work's popularity."
